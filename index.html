I am an Editorial Intern at St. Louis Magazine, where I cover arts and culture. I am currently a rising senior at The New School in Manhattan, studying Journalism and Design with a minor in Politics.

Before transferring to The New School, I studied Fashion Design at Parsons School of Design for a year and a half. This experience gave me a creative foundation across a range of disciplines, including video production, graphic design, technical sewing and drawing. In 2025, I also wrote for the features desk at The New School Free Press.

I recently completed a semester abroad at King's College London, where I studied Politics, Media and Culture. My interests as a journalist centre on the relationship between politics, culture and everyday life. I enjoy exploring a wide range of subjects and believe in the power of thoughtful storytelling, with journalistic ethics and integrity at the centre of my work.
